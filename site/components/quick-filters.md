# Quick Filters

One-tap filter **presets** that give users faster access.

Common filters like *Assigned to Me*, *My Company*, and *Due in 7 Days* — applied in a single tap.

::: info Local for now
Currently, Quick Filters are determined locally on-device. In the future, Quick Filters could be shared between Web and Mobile.
:::
